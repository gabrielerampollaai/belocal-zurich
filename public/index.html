For Newcomers

Your first ninety days

Week 1

Register at the Kreisbüro

Within 14 days of arriving. Bring passport, rental contract and work contract.

Week 2

Get a Halbtax & ZVV pass

Half price on every Swiss train. Locals consider it non-negotiable.

Month 1

Choose your Krankenkasse

Health insurance is mandatory within three months, and backdated. Compare before you sign.

Month 3

Find your Badi & your Verein

A swimming spot and a club. That's how you become a Zürcher.
